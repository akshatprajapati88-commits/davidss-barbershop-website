What to know

Three brothers,
one standard.

Efficient, unpretentious, and quietly excellent — the kind of shop a neighbourhood builds a habit around.

01

Family-owned

Run by a team of experienced brothers who take their time with detail and precision. Twenty years of long-term local trust, built one haircut at a time.

02

Open seven days

Eight in the morning to seven at night, Monday through Friday and all day Sunday. The only day the door is shut is Saturday.

03

Honest prices

Quality haircuts at reasonable prices. No upsell, no theatre — just a great cut for a great price, the thing reviewers mention most.
